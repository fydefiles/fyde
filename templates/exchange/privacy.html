WORKING POLICIES · NOVEMBER 7, 2026

Know where your
files go.

Fyde stores uploaded original bytes, extracted text, document metadata, contacts, permissions, transfer records, and email verification messages to operate your workspace. Search runs on the application server. Fyde does not provide end-to-end encryption; your hosting provider's disk and database encryption must be configured separately.

Sharing

The backend checks the owner, verified recipient endpoint, exact version, permission, expiry, and pause controls before delivery and download. The email provider receives the destination address and a recipient-bound link, rather than the original file attachment. Recipients pass email verification before downloading. Downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Business agents

Business agents work with opted-in feedback, public research, sanitized source code, and synthetic fixtures. Customer files, extracted text, private storage, and customer database credentials are excluded from builder inputs. Customer and company records use separate databases. Production deployments must keep the builder sandbox and its credentials separate as described in the operator runbook.

Feedback

Public feature requests may appear on the first-party feedback board after sanitization. Private support stays private. Exclude document text, passwords, financial information, and other private details. Automated text redaction can miss information; inspect what you submit. Reddit and Instagram integrations require their own authorization.

Product measurement

Campaign-tagged product links record first-party visit and signup counts, with a 24-hour session attribution window. The company receives aggregate activation counts. Private document text, filenames, and recipient addresses are excluded. Shared links and cross-device visits make attribution uncertain; exact per-post revenue is not claimed.

Retention, export, and deletion

Original-file export remains available after subscription cancellation. You can export account metadata from Settings. Deleting a document requires your password and deletes its original bytes and searchable text, while revoking sharing links. Minimal audit and usage records remain. Automatic retention expiry, complete account deletion, and backup erasure are pending operator policy and implementation. Backups must follow the approved retention policy before public launch.
