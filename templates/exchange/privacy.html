WORKING POLICIES · NOVEMBER 7, 2026

Know where your
files go.

Fyde stores uploaded original bytes, extracted text, document metadata, contacts, case assignments, relationship labels, permissions, private chat history, transfer records, received-file records, hashed device/agent credentials, and email/SMS verification messages to operate your workspace. Search runs on the application server. When semantic search is configured, Fyde sends extracted text passages and search queries to its private EmbeddingGemma 2 service in Google Cloud. Fyde stores the resulting embeddings in the customer database alongside extracted text. Fyde does not provide end-to-end encryption; your hosting provider's disk and database encryption must be configured separately.

Sharing

The backend checks the owner, verified recipient endpoint, exact version, permission, expiry, and pause controls before delivery and download. Fyde delivers to a verified account’s Received page by default. Optional email or SMS providers receive the selected destination and a recipient-bound download link. Recipients sign in to the matching verified account or verify the selected email or phone before downloading inside Fyde. The inbox references the owner’s original version and keeps its expiry and revocation controls. Downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Case permissions and personal agents

Relationship labels help find a contact; they do not establish professional status or grant access. You can explicitly authorize a verified contact to request documents assigned to a case, including selected document types and, if you choose, future files, future versions or sensitive files. Requests within that permission can deliver without another approval. A recipient agent credential covers one received case permission and cannot change permissions or browse unrelated files. Revoke the case permission or pause outgoing sharing to stop future releases and downloads. Revoking one transfer does not cancel an independently active case permission.

Chat history stays in your customer workspace and is shared across your signed-in web and mobile sessions. Recent choices can provide 15-minute context; turn that off in Settings or Account. The current assistant uses bounded commands on the server. The configured semantic search service processes passages to find relevant files; its results cannot grant access or authorize sharing. Native sessions use device secure storage; downloaded copies may be exported through the operating system and cannot be recalled.

Business agents

Business agents work with opted-in feedback, public research, sanitized source code, and synthetic fixtures. Customer files, extracted text, private storage, and customer database credentials are excluded from builder inputs. Customer and company records use separate databases. Production deployments must keep the builder sandbox and its credentials separate as described in the operator runbook.

Feedback

Public feature requests may appear on the first-party feedback board after sanitization. Private support stays private. Exclude document text, passwords, financial information, and other private details. Automated text redaction can miss information; inspect what you submit. Reddit and Instagram integrations require their own authorization.

Product measurement

Campaign-tagged product links record first-party visit and signup counts, with a 24-hour session attribution window. The company receives aggregate activation counts. Private document text, filenames, and recipient addresses are excluded. Shared links and cross-device visits make attribution uncertain; exact per-post revenue is not claimed.

Retention, export, and deletion

Original-file export remains available after subscription cancellation. You can export account metadata from Settings. Deleting a document requires your password and deletes its original bytes and searchable text, while revoking sharing links. Minimal audit and usage records remain. Automatic retention expiry, complete account deletion, and backup erasure are pending operator policy and implementation. Backups must follow the approved retention policy before public launch.
